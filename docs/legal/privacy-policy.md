# Ekalight Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 17, 2026_

Ekalight ("we", "us", the "App") helps you scan trading cards, see prices, and track
your collection. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

> **Before publishing:** host this page at a public HTTPS URL and put that URL in
> App Store Connect (App Privacy → Privacy Policy URL).

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email address and
display name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email and name from
that provider (with Sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email).

**Card scans.** When you scan a card, the App captures a photo of the card and sends it
to our servers to identify the card and return pricing. Scan images are stored to
operate and improve recognition. They are private to your account.

**Collection data.** Cards you add, their grades/conditions/quantities, and any
purchase/sale transactions you log.

**Usage data.** Basic analytics about how the App is used (screens viewed, features
used, crashes) to improve the product.

## How we use information

- Identify scanned cards and show prices.
- Maintain your account and your collection.
- Operate, secure, and improve the App and recognition models.
- Provide support.

## Third parties we share data with

We use these service providers to run the App; they process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — authentication and database (your account and collection data).
- **Google / Apple** — sign-in, if you choose those options.
- **Google Cloud** — hosting and storage of scan images.
- **Scrydex** — card catalog and pricing lookups.
- **PostHog** — product analytics.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

You can **delete your account at any time inside the App** (Account → Delete Account).
This permanently deletes your account, your collection, and your scan data, and removes
your sign-in record. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

You may also email us at team@ekalight.com to request deletion.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from
them.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport/HTTPS, access controls) to
protect your data. No method is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
