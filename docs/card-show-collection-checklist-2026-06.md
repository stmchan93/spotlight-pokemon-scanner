## 🟢 Older sets — for COVERAGE (grab a spread across eras, EN + JP)
The lists above fix top‑1 on what we scan *now* (recent-heavy). This section fills a different
gap: our training data is recent-heavy, so the model is **thin on older eras** — it'll miss
vintage if/when we scan it. Grab a handful from **each era** (breadth, not depth):
- [ ] **2023 Scarlet & Violet:** Paldea Evolved · Obsidian Flames · Paradox Rift · 151

- [ ] **Japanese:** same eras — grab JP singles from each.

## The two rules (if you forget everything else)
1. **New sets → go DEEP** (especially Japanese) — that's where the scanner misses today.
2. **Old sets → go BROAD** — a few from every era (EN + JP) so the model isn't blind to vintage.
