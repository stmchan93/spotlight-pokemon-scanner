When do I use each button?
  • Pick a card (AI pick / a candidate / search) — it's a raw single and you can identify it. This is the normal case.
  • Not in list (n) — it's a real raw card, but the right one isn't offered and search can't find it. Right card, missing options.
  • Unclear (u) — it's a card but you genuinely can't tell which (blurry / glare / cut off). Comes back to you later.
  • Not a card · slab/junk (g) — it's a graded slab in a plastic case, or not a card at all (a blanket, a hand, a booster wrapper, an empty shot). Drops it for everyone — never trained on.
  • Skip (s) — you just want to pass it to someone else for now. Comes back to you later.
A blanket (or anything that isn't a card) → Not a card · slab/junk (g).
